## Manual steps on an iPhone PWA

**Nothing here is verified on a device or in a browser.** The fixes are reasoned from CSS (the `(hover: hover)` media feature, flex layout of the top block, sticky positioning), not observed.

1. Press, with at least 6 orders: have a cashier cancel one order (or cancel one yourself from another device) so "Order #N cancelled by …" appears. Scroll the list to the 4th card. The notice stays under the header. Its countdown bar shrinks; Dismiss removes it.
2. Press: with the notice still up, tap Claim on a lower card while offline or on a slow network so an error toast shows ("Couldn't update order #N…"). It is under the header too, and it does not cover a dialog (open "Cancel order" to check that the dialog is above it).
3. Press: tap "Cancel order", then "Keep order". The button goes back to grey (not red). Tap Dismiss on a notice: no red flash left behind.
4. Cashier, Queue with a Ready order: tap "Cancel order", then "Keep order". The button is red outline only, not filled. Tap "✓ Picked up" with the network off: the error shows under the tabs while the list is scrolled. Switch to New order: the toast may still be visible there for its few seconds.
5. Admin Orders, Staff, Events: cause an error while scrolled down (offline, tap an action). The toast sticks at the top of the scrolling area.
6. Admin Designs: tap "Replace front" and "Replace back": the text does not turn red after the tap.
7. Cashier, New order, Bundle at 360 px and at 390 px wide (browser devtools device mode is only an approximation): two tiles per row; each whole tile is tappable; photos are still recognisable at 96 px high. At 430 px two per row. On an iPad or at 900 px and wider: as before (160 px minimum).
8. Section labels ("Ready for pickup · N", "Shirt color", "Select your station") are 13 px and readable at arm's length.
9. Login: pick a role and a person. The name shows at 22 px above the PIN dots with "Enter PIN" under it, "← Back" above both. Back goes to the start screen. Try a long name (wraps, does not overflow).
